About Godrej Sarjapur Road

Godrej Sarjapur Road is a premium residential project by Godrej Properties, located on Sarjapur Road in South Bangalore — one of the city’s most sought-after residential corridors. This pre-launch development offers thoughtfully designed 1, 2, and 3 BHK luxury apartments with sizes ranging from 1,100 sq. ft. to 2,150 sq. ft. Designed for modern urban living, the project combines elegant architecture, quality construction, and well-planned spaces to deliver a comfortable and stylish lifestyle for homebuyers.
