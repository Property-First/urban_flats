Project Overview

Panvel is fast emerging as one of the most promising real estate destinations in Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR), and L&T Crestoria Panvel stands tall as a landmark residential development redefining luxury living. Developed by L&T Realty, this iconic township brings together world-class design, expansive green spaces, and future-ready connectivity in one of Navi Mumbai's most strategic locations.

Spread across a massive 35-acre freehold land parcel, L&T Crestoria Estates Panvel is not just a housing project—it's a lifestyle destination designed for modern families, professionals, and long-term investors seeking quality, trust, and appreciation.

L&T Crestoria Panvel is thoughtfully planned to offer a perfect balance between urban convenience and natural serenity. Inspired by European Spanish architecture, the project promises an international living experience complemented by scenic Matheran hill views.
