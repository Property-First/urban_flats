About Godrej Vanantara

Godrej Vanantara is the new luxury Apartment project launched just off Bannerghatta Road, South Bangalore. It's right next to Greenwood School in Dinnepalya. The residential enclave is a massive-scale development spread across 33+ acres featuring ultra-premium living. The project offers spacious low-density homes ranging from 2, 3, 3.5, 4 BHK Apartments with luxurious features.

Beautiful landscapes with over 80% open spaces, serene views, and plenty of green cover make Godrej Vanantara special & elite. The builder guarantees a quality living experience with brilliant resort-style architecture and an equivalent lifestyle.

Godrej Vanantara Location has excellent connectivity & it is located just off Bannerghatta Road on Dinnepalya Road, right next to Greenwood High school campus. Close to NICE Road Junction, Gottigere, ECity, Kanakapura Road, JP Nagar. The project offers 70,000 sqft clubhouse with world-class amenities.

Note: This project is RERA approved. Possession from May 2031 onwards.
