Specifications

Structure

RCC framed structure of 1 Basement + Ground + 37/38 stories.

Flooring

Foyer/Living/Dining/Bedrooms: Vitrified / Ceramic tile flooring and skirting. Bathroom/Kitchen: Ceramic tile flooring.

Doors & Windows

Main Door: Timber frame with timber architrave and flush shutter. Internal doors: Timber frame and architrave. Windows: UPVC aluminum glazed windows.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Sanitary fixtures of reputed make in all bathrooms. Chromium-plated fittings in all bathrooms. Rainwater harvesting pipes included.

Security

3 elevators of reputed make and quality in each residential wing. The entire project is secured with a compound wall and entrances will be manned by security. CCTV surveillance at all vantage points.

Electrical

BESCOM power supply of 5 KW supply for each home. Modular switches of reputed make. Good quality concealed copper wiring of approved make. ELCB and MCB with independent EB meter of approved make. Standby power of 1 KW for apartments and 100% power backup for common area facilities.

About SOBHA Limited

Trusted Real Estate Developer

Founded in the year 1995, Sobha Limited is one of the largest real estate development company in India. It's a Rs. 22 billion company based out of Bangalore and having projects spread across 13 states and 24 cities.

Sobha Limited is primarily focused on the development of residential and contractual projects. The residential projects range from villas and row houses to plotted development, presidential apartments, luxury, and super luxury apartments. Every Sobha project comes equipped with every major amenity including shopping complex, clubhouse, and swimming pool.

Presently, almost 35 residential projects are under construction aggregating to 27.32 million sq. ft. of space under development. 18.90 million sq. ft. of saleable space in addition to ongoing contractual projects adding up to almost 9.50 million square feet of area.
