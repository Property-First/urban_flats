Project Overview

L&T Electronic City is the brand new luxury residential apartment project launched in Electronic City, Bangalore, developed by the trusted name L&T Realty.

The residential enclave L&T Electronic City features the very best in L&T Realty's luxury living segment, spread across a prime location. The project offers spacious 3, 4, and 5 BHK apartments with luxurious features and modern designs.

One of the many USPs of the project is that each apartment is designed with spacious balconies, broad decks, and large windows to maximize natural light and ventilation.

Beautiful landscapes all around L&T Electronic City, with significant open spaces and green covers, make it more special and elite. The project emphasizes sustainable living with eco-friendly features and green building practices.

The builder, L&T Realty, is renowned for quality construction and is guaranteed to bring a premium living experience to the community of Electronic City, Bangalore, with brilliant architecture and an equivalent lifestyle in L&T Electronic City.
