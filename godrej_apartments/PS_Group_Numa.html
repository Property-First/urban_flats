THE PROJECT

About PS Group Numa

An ultra-luxury high-rise residential development by PS Group, located on EM Bypass near ITC, Kolkata.

PS Group Numa is an iconic ultra-luxury residential development by PS Group, designed for homeowners who seek expansive living spaces, architectural excellence, and an elevated lifestyle experience. Strategically located on EM Bypass near ITC, the project offers seamless connectivity to Kolkata's major business hubs, premium schools, renowned hospitals, luxury hotels, shopping destinations, and entertainment districts.

Spread across 4 acres of freehold land, PS Group Numa features two landmark towers with a magnificent B+G+42 elevation. With only 155 exclusive residences, including spacious 4 BHK and 5 BHK simplex apartments and 6 BHK duplex residences, the development delivers a highly private and low-density luxury living environment.

The residences are crafted with exceptional attention to detail, featuring 11.5-foot ceiling heights, expansive layouts ranging from 4,800 to 8,200 sq.ft., premium specifications, and dedicated servant quarters (SQ + ST). Residents also enjoy covered independent EV-ready parking along with mechanical parking options, ensuring convenience and future-ready mobility.

PS Group Numa offers an extraordinary collection of lifestyle amenities, including a 70,000 sq.ft. elevated lifestyle podium, 6 exclusive sky lounges, 17,000 sq.ft. grand banquet hall, 12,000 sq.ft. luxury gym with café & juice bar, advanced wellness facilities such as blue and red light therapy, couple spa experiences, an acoustic after-party lounge, and a 15,000 sq.ft. dedicated kids' play zone, creating a truly world-class luxury ecosystem within the development.

With its prestigious EM Bypass address, limited inventory, expansive residences, exceptional ceiling heights, and internationally inspired lifestyle amenities, PS Group Numa stands as one of Kolkata's most exclusive luxury residential offerings, making it an exceptional opportunity for discerning homeowners and long-term investors alike.
