THE PROJECT

About Srijan Palladina

An iconic luxury serviced apartment development by Srijan, located behind ITC on EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Srijan Palladina is an ultra-premium residential development by Srijan, offering an exceptional blend of luxury, privacy, and contemporary design in one of Kolkata’s most sought-after locations. Strategically located behind ITC on EM Bypass, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to major business districts, premium schools, hospitals, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs across the city.

Spread across 3.5 acres, Srijan Palladina is an exclusive single-tower development with a striking B+G+43 elevation and only 105 luxury residences, ensuring a low-density and highly private living experience. The project offers spacious 4 BHK simplex and duplex residences, along with select 6 BHK residences on request, featuring expansive layouts, high ceilings, abundant natural light, and premium specifications.

Designed for refined urban living, the project includes thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities such as a 43rd-floor sky lounge, a North-East temple, and a dedicated separate parking block. With 11.3-foot floor-to-floor ceiling height, VRV provision, and premium servant quarter configurations, every residence is crafted for comfort, exclusivity, and elegance.

Scheduled for RERA completion in January 2030 with an approximate fit-out timeline of January 2029, Srijan Palladina represents a rare opportunity to own a landmark luxury residence on EM Bypass. Its prime location, exclusive single-tower design, and limited inventory make it an exceptional choice for discerning homeowners and long-term investors.
