THE PROJECT

About Salarpuria Inspire

An exclusive premium residential development by Salarpuria, located at Action Area 2D, Newtown, beside Silver Oak Estate.

Salarpuria Inspire is a premium residential development by Salarpuria, thoughtfully planned for homebuyers seeking spacious residences, privacy, comfort, and a well-connected lifestyle. Located at Action Area 2D, Newtown, beside Silver Oak Estate, the project enjoys convenient connectivity to key destinations across Newtown and Kolkata, with easy access to the airport, City Centre 2, and the upcoming metro station.

Spread across 8.4 acres, Salarpuria Inspire comprises 14 residential towers with a B+G+12 elevation. The development features approximately 700 residences, offering spacious 3 BHK, 3.5 BHK and 4 BHK simplex residences. Apartment sizes range from approximately 1,450 to 2,050 sq. ft., providing thoughtfully planned layouts suited to modern family living.

The residences are designed to offer a comfortable and premium living experience, with basement parking and spacious home configurations. The project offers 2 parking options with select 4 BHK residences, while the development has been planned with multiple open spaces and dedicated amenity zones to create a balanced residential environment.

One of the key highlights of Salarpuria Inspire is its thoughtfully designed landscape and amenity planning. The development features lush landscaped gardens, jogging and walking tracks, children's play areas, outdoor fitness zones, and meditation and reflexology gardens. Dedicated spaces for senior citizens further enhance the community-oriented living experience.

The project also features a premium multi-activity clubhouse with a range of indoor amenities, including a swimming pool and deck, state-of-the-art gymnasium, banquet/multipurpose hall, indoor games room, co-working and reading lounge, and yoga & wellness studio. With its strategic Newtown location, spacious homes, landscaped surroundings, extensive amenities, and connectivity to major city destinations, Salarpuria Inspire presents an attractive premium residential opportunity in Kolkata.
