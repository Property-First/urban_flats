THE PROJECT

About Srijan Optima

A premium residential development by Srijan, located at Rajarhat Chowmatha, Kolkata.

Srijan Optima is a premium residential development by Srijan, thoughtfully designed for homebuyers seeking spacious apartments, modern amenities, and excellent connectivity. Located at Rajarhat Chowmatha, Kolkata, the project enjoys a strategic location with seamless access to New Town, Salt Lake Sector V, the airport, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and major commercial hubs.

Spread across 11.3 acres of freehold land, Srijan Optima comprises 14 residential towers and 1 retail tower with a G+18 elevation. The development features approximately 1,400 premium residences, offering thoughtfully planned 2, 3, and 4 BHK apartments with spacious layouts ranging from approximately 921 to 2,596 sq. ft..

Designed for a comfortable and contemporary lifestyle, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height, quality finishes, and efficient space planning. The project offers multiple parking options, including ground and multi-level car parking (MLCP), along with a dedicated podium recreation zone that enhances the overall residential experience.

One of the defining highlights of Srijan Optima is its expansive landscape and open planning. The development offers over 70% open space, a podium of approximately 1 lakh sq. ft., and 75+ lifestyle amenities, creating a vibrant community environment with abundant greenery and recreational spaces.

Residents can enjoy a wide range of facilities including a swimming pool, café, gymnasium, landscaped gardens, clubhouse, indoor and outdoor recreational areas, jogging tracks, and family-friendly leisure spaces. With its spacious homes, extensive amenities, landscaped surroundings, and excellent connectivity, Srijan Optima offers an exceptional residential opportunity in Rajarhat, Kolkata.
