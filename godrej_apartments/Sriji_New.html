THE PROJECT

About Sriji New

A premium residential development by Sriji Group, located in New Tangra beside Boulevard, off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Sriji New is a thoughtfully planned residential project by Sriji Group, designed for families seeking spacious homes, modern comforts, and excellent urban connectivity. Located in New Tangra beside Boulevard, off EM Bypass, the project enjoys seamless access to Kolkata’s major business districts, schools, hospitals, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs.

Spread across 2 acres of freehold land, the development comprises 2 premium residential towers with a G+12 elevation. The project offers well-designed 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex apartments with spacious layouts ranging from 1408 sq.ft. to 1913 sq.ft., ensuring comfortable living for modern families.

Every residence is planned with an emphasis on natural light, ventilation, functionality, and contemporary design. The homes are crafted to provide a balanced lifestyle with generous living spaces, quality construction, and premium finishes that enhance everyday comfort.

Residents will enjoy a host of modern lifestyle amenities and community-focused facilities that promote recreation, wellness, and social interaction. The project is envisioned as a secure and well-connected residential community offering convenience and a superior quality of life.

With its strategic location near EM Bypass, spacious apartment configurations, premium planning, and excellent connectivity, Sriji New presents a strong opportunity for both end-users and long-term investors looking for a premium residential address in Kolkata.
