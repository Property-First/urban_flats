THE PROJECT

About the Upcoming Project

An upcoming luxury residential development by NPR & Purti Realty & Shriji, located on Canal South Road near Paridhan, off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

The upcoming project by NPR & Purti Realty & Shriji is a premium residential development planned for homebuyers seeking spacious residences, refined specifications, and a well-connected address in Kolkata. Located on Canal South Road, near Paridhan, off EM Bypass, the project offers convenient access to major roads, commercial destinations, lifestyle hubs, and key areas across the city.

Spread across approximately 14 acres of freehold land, the development comprises 6 towers with a proposed B+G+27 elevation, with 33 floors proposed. With around 450 residences, the project offers spacious 4 BHK and 5 BHK simplex residences, along with selected duplex options designed for larger families seeking generous living spaces.

The residences are planned in multiple spacious configurations ranging from approximately 2,750 sq. ft. to 6,900 sq. ft.. The project also offers 11.5-foot ceiling heights, independent parking, and servant quarters with servant toilet facilities, adding convenience and comfort to the overall residential experience.

Residents can enjoy an extensive collection of lifestyle amenities, including multiple banquet halls, a luxury banquet hall with terrace and private swimming pool, temperature-controlled indoor swimming pool, outdoor swimming pool, pickleball courts, badminton courts, squash courts, bowling area, and karaoke room. Each tower is planned with 4 passenger lifts and 2 service lifts.

A key highlight of the development is its approximately 1.25 lakh sq. ft. podium level, connecting all six towers and creating an expansive lifestyle zone for residents. The project is also located approximately 700 metres from Chingrighata Crossing, adding to its connectivity advantage.

With its expansive land parcel, spacious 4 & 5 BHK residences, premium lifestyle amenities, independent parking, and strategic location off EM Bypass, the upcoming NPR & Purti Realty & Shriji development presents an attractive opportunity for buyers seeking a large-format luxury residence in Kolkata.
