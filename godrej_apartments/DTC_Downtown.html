THE PROJECT

About DTC Downtown

A premium residential development by DTC, strategically located in Rajarhat, Kolkata, just 4 minutes from Chowmatha.

DTC Downtown is a premium residential development by DTC, designed for homebuyers seeking spacious residences, modern conveniences, and a well-connected urban lifestyle. Located in Rajarhat, just 4 minutes from Chowmatha, the project offers convenient connectivity to key destinations, business hubs, retail destinations, and everyday essentials across New Town and Rajarhat.

Spread across 10 acres of freehold land, DTC Downtown comprises six premium towers with a B+G+29 elevation. The development features a total of 1,313 residences, with Phase 1 comprising 656 units. The project offers thoughtfully planned 2, 3 and 4 BHK simplex residences, with sizes ranging from approximately 965 to 2,010 sq. ft.

Designed to provide a comfortable and contemporary lifestyle, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height. The development provides multiple parking options, including basement, podium and ground-level parking. With 8 flats per floor, the towers are planned to offer a balanced combination of accessibility, privacy, and efficient space utilisation.

One of the key highlights of DTC Downtown is its extensive lifestyle infrastructure, anchored by an impressive 110,000 sq. ft. clubhouse. Residents can enjoy a range of recreational and professional amenities, including a bowling arcade, café and co-working space, creating a vibrant environment for relaxation, socialising, and work-from-home needs.

With its 10-acre freehold land parcel, six-tower configuration, spacious 2, 3 and 4 BHK residences, premium clubhouse, and strategic location just 4 minutes from Chowmatha, DTC Downtown presents an attractive residential opportunity in Rajarhat. The project combines contemporary urban living with thoughtfully planned amenities and generous residential spaces.
