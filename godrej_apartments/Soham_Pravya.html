THE PROJECT

About Soham Pravya

A premium low-density residential development by Soham, located at Jabardanga, Rajarhat, Kolkata.

Soham Pravya is a thoughtfully designed premium residential development by Soham, created for homebuyers seeking spacious apartments, privacy, natural ventilation, and modern lifestyle amenities. Located in Jabardanga, Rajarhat, Kolkata, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to New Town, Sector V, EM Bypass, the airport corridor, reputed schools, hospitals, shopping destinations, and major IT hubs, making it an ideal address for both families and professionals.

Spread across 3.36 acres of freehold land, Soham Pravya comprises seven residential towers, with two towers currently launched. The development features only 104 premium residences, offering spacious 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T, and 4 BHK + 3T simplex apartments with thoughtfully planned layouts ranging from approximately 1,389 to 2,143 sq.ft. (SBU).

Designed for an elevated lifestyle, every residence features a generous 10-foot ceiling height, enhancing natural light, ventilation, and the overall sense of space. The apartments are truly three-sides-open, ensuring superior cross ventilation and abundant daylight throughout the day, while also being Vastu-compliant for harmonious living.

The project is planned as a low-density gated community, offering greater privacy and a peaceful residential environment. Residents can enjoy premium lifestyle amenities including a 30,000+ sq.ft. clubhouse, an infinity-edge swimming pool, a fully equipped gymnasium, landscaped open spaces, recreational zones, and a dedicated co-working space for modern professionals and remote working convenience.

Soham Pravya further enhances convenience with basement multi-level car parking (MLCP), finished residences, and carefully planned infrastructure designed for comfortable long-term living. With its spacious homes, premium amenities, low-density planning, and strategic location in the rapidly developing Rajarhat-New Town corridor, Soham Pravya presents an exceptional residential opportunity in Kolkata.

MASTER PLAN

Thoughtfully Planned for Premium Living

Explore the master plan of Soham Pravya, Jabardanga, Rajarhat, Kolkata.

Soham Pravya is spread across 3.36 acres of freehold land and comprises seven residential towers, with two towers currently launched. The development has been thoughtfully planned as a low-density premium residential community, offering only 104 exclusive residences surrounded by landscaped open spaces and modern lifestyle infrastructure.

The project offers spacious 3 BHK + 2T, 3 BHK + 3T, and 4 BHK + 3T simplex residences, with sizes ranging from approximately 1,389 to 2,143 sq.ft. (SBU). Every apartment is designed as a truly three-sides-open residence, ensuring abundant natural light, cross ventilation, and enhanced privacy for residents.

A key highlight of the master plan is its 30,000+ sq.ft. premium clubhouse, complemented by an infinity-edge swimming pool, landscaped gardens, recreational zones, and a dedicated co-working space. The development also includes basement multi-level car parking (MLCP) and well-planned internal circulation designed for convenience and safety.

The residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height and are Vastu-compliant, creating a bright, spacious, and harmonious living environment. The overall master plan emphasizes low-density living, open green areas, premium amenities, and efficient infrastructure, offering an ideal balance of comfort, privacy, and long-term value in the rapidly developing Rajarhat–New Town corridor.
