THE PROJECT

About Srijan Braj

An ultra-luxury residential development by Srijan Realty, located off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Srijan Braj is an exclusive ultra-luxury residential development by Srijan Realty, thoughtfully designed for discerning homeowners seeking expansive living spaces, privacy, and a premium lifestyle. Strategically located off EM Bypass, behind Inia Juneja Group, the project offers excellent connectivity to key destinations across Kolkata.

Spread across 9 acres of freehold land, Srijan Braj comprises six residential towers with elevations of G+31, G+32 and G+34. The development features approximately 520 residences, offering spacious 4 BHK + 4T and 5 BHK + 4T simplex configurations with sizes ranging from approximately 2,700 sq. ft. to 3,900 sq. ft.

Designed for grand and comfortable living, the residences feature an impressive 11.5-foot ceiling height, spacious layouts, premium specifications, and dedicated servant quarters with servant toilet (SQ + ST). The project also provides multiple parking options and an approximate maintenance charge of ₹6 per sq. ft. on SBU.

One of the defining highlights of Srijan Braj is its expansive 1 lakh+ sq. ft. podium and distinctive jungle-themed project concept. Combined with a wide range of thoughtfully planned amenities, the development creates a unique residential environment that blends luxury, space, and a nature-inspired lifestyle.

With its strategic Off EM Bypass location, expansive 9-acre freehold land parcel, spacious 4 and 5 BHK residences, six high-rise towers, and distinctive lifestyle features, Srijan Braj presents a premium residential opportunity for homebuyers seeking an elevated lifestyle in Kolkata.
