THE PROJECT

About Godrej Blue

A landmark luxury residential address by Godrej Properties, set in the heart of New Alipore, Kolkata.

Godrej Blue is a prestigious residential development by Godrej Properties, offering a perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and contemporary living in New Alipore, Kolkata. Located at Mahabirtalla on BL Saha Road, just a 5-minute walk from Tollygunge Phari, the project provides seamless connectivity to South Kolkata's major business districts, educational institutions, healthcare centres, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs.

Spread across 7.4 acres, Godrej Blue features 480 premium residences across 7 elegantly designed towers with G+19 and G+21 configurations. The development offers thoughtfully planned 3 and 4 BHK luxury apartments with spacious layouts, elegant interiors, abundant natural light, and excellent cross ventilation, creating an ideal lifestyle for families, professionals, and discerning homeowners.

The project is surrounded by approximately 60% open green space, including 2 acres of podium-level landscaping and a 1-acre natural and beautified lake, creating a serene environment rarely found in the city. Residents can enjoy world-class amenities such as an Olympic-size infinity swimming pool, indoor temperature-controlled jacuzzi, luxury spa, squash court, basketball court, gymnasium, Zumba studio, guest suites, and an independent premium clubhouse.

Designed for those who appreciate life's finer privileges, Godrej Blue offers a rare combination of private greens, timeless architecture, and premium lifestyle amenities. The project is scheduled for possession in December 2028, making it one of New Alipore's most distinguished luxury residential developments and an excellent opportunity for long-term value appreciation.
