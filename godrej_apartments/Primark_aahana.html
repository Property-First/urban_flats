THE PROJECT

About Primark Aahana

An exclusive boutique luxury residential development by Primark, located in Kasba, just 400 metres from Acropolis Mall, Off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Primark Aahana is an exclusive boutique residential development by Primark, thoughtfully designed for homeowners seeking spacious residences, privacy, and an elevated lifestyle. Located in Kasba, just 400 metres from Acropolis Mall, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to EM Bypass, Ruby More, Ballygunge, Gariahat, and other key destinations across Kolkata.

Spread across 56 Kottha of freehold land, Primark Aahana features a single G+16 tower with only 30 exclusive residences. The limited number of homes creates a private, low-density community, with just 2 residences per floor. The project offers spacious 4 BHK, 4.5 BHK, and 5 BHK configurations in both simplex and duplex formats.

Residences range from approximately 3,300 sq. ft. to 5,700 sq. ft. SBU, offering generous living spaces for families who value comfort and exclusivity. Every residence is designed as a 3-side open, Vaastu-compliant home, complemented by impressive 10.5-foot ceiling heights and premium specifications including vitrified tiles and VRV air-conditioning.

One of the defining highlights of Primark Aahana is its focus on privacy and lifestyle within a boutique development. The project features a dedicated G+1 clubhouse with amenities including a swimming pool, gymnasium, and banquet hall. With approximately 70% open area and only large-format residences, the development offers a more exclusive living environment with a carefully curated community.

The project also provides MLCP parking, servant quarters, and other essential conveniences for modern luxury living. With its strategic Off EM Bypass location, proximity to Acropolis Mall, spacious residences, limited inventory, and boutique character, Primark Aahana presents an exclusive residential opportunity in Kasba, Kolkata, for discerning homebuyers seeking space, privacy, and connectivity.
