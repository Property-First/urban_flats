About Merlin F Residencies

A fashion-inspired premium residential township by Merlin Group, located at Jabardanga, Rajarhat, Kolkata.

Merlin F Residencies is a premium branded residential development by Merlin Group, created for homebuyers seeking spacious residences, contemporary design, and world-class lifestyle amenities. Located in Jabardanga, Rajarhat, Kolkata, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to New Town, Salt Lake Sector V, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, leading hospitals, shopping malls, and educational institutions, making it one of the most well-connected residential destinations in the city.

Spread across an expansive 15-acre freehold land parcel, Merlin F Residencies comprises 11 residential towers with B+G+12 and B+G+13 elevations. The development features 789 thoughtfully designed simplex apartments, offering spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK residences with well-planned layouts ranging from approximately 960 to 1,570 sq. ft..

Designed for modern urban living, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height, premium finished interiors, and multiple parking options including basement parking, ground independent parking, and back-to-back ground parking. Every home is planned to provide ample natural light, ventilation, and functional living spaces for contemporary families.

One of the standout highlights of Merlin F Residencies is its exceptional lifestyle infrastructure. Residents can enjoy a luxurious G+5 clubhouse, a spectacular two-tier swimming pool, and a unique rooftop cloud forest with interconnected rooftop spaces, creating an elevated living experience that blends architecture, greenery, and leisure.

Merlin F Residencies is further distinguished by its collaboration with F-TV (Fashion TV) as the styling partner, bringing fashion-inspired design aesthetics and contemporary lifestyle elements into the residential environment. With its spacious homes, premium amenities, landscaped open spaces, and strategic Rajarhat location, Merlin F Residencies offers a compelling opportunity for both end-users and long-term real estate investors in Kolkata.
