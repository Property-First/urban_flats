THE PROJECT

About Purti Anaaya

An exclusive boutique residential development by Purti Realty & NPR, located on Pagala Daga Road, just before PS Jiva, off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Purti Anaaya is a premium boutique residential development by Purti Realty & NPR, designed for homebuyers seeking spacious residences, privacy, and a refined urban lifestyle. Strategically located on Pagala Daga Road, just before PS Jiva, off EM Bypass, the project offers convenient connectivity to key destinations across Kolkata.

Spread across 90 Cottah of freehold land, Purti Anaaya features an exclusive single residential tower with a G+23 elevation. With only 92 premium residences, the development offers a low-density living environment with spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex residences.

The residences are thoughtfully planned in multiple configurations, including 3 BHK + 3T, 4 BHK + 3T, and 4 BHK + 4T, with apartment sizes ranging from approximately 1,793 to 2,367 sq. ft.. A generous 10-foot ceiling height further enhances the sense of space, natural light, and comfort within the residences.

Purti Anaaya also features a dedicated MLCP parking block with G+3 parking levels, while the upper floors are planned for lifestyle amenities. The project offers all essential lifestyle amenities within the development, allowing residents to enjoy convenience and recreation without compromising on privacy.

With its strategic location off EM Bypass, limited number of residences, spacious simplex homes, freehold land parcel, and boutique single-tower design, Purti Anaaya presents an attractive opportunity for homebuyers looking for premium residential living in Kolkata.
