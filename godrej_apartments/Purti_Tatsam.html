THE PROJECT

About Purti Tatsam

A premium residential development by Purti, located at Jabardanga, Rajarhat, near F Residencies, Kolkata.

Purti Tatsam is a thoughtfully planned residential development by Purti, designed for homebuyers seeking spacious apartments, privacy, and a premium lifestyle in one of Rajarhat's rapidly developing neighborhoods. Located at Jabardanga, Rajarhat, near F Residencies, the project offers excellent connectivity to New Town, EM Bypass, Salt Lake, the airport, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and major commercial hubs across Kolkata.

Spread across 2.33 acres of freehold land, Purti Tatsam comprises three residential towers with a B+G+12 elevation, with an additional future tower planned. The development features only 189 premium apartments, offering spacious 3 and 4 BHK simplex residences with thoughtfully designed layouts ranging from approximately 1,278 to 1,788 sq. ft..

Designed for comfortable family living, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height, premium finishes, and large balconies that enhance natural light and ventilation. The project offers multiple parking options, including MLCP, basement, and open parking, along with efficient planning that ensures a low-density living environment.

One of the key highlights of Purti Tatsam is its distinctive 4-tier amenity concept, with lifestyle zones thoughtfully distributed across the ground level, clubhouse, podium, and rooftop. Residents can enjoy amenities such as a rooftop swimming pool, gymnasium, community hall, landscaped open spaces, and dedicated leisure areas, creating a well-balanced urban lifestyle.

The project is strategically positioned in a rapidly appreciating pocket of Rajarhat, with several upcoming residential and infrastructure developments in the surrounding area. Since nearby projects such as F Residencies command significantly higher pricing, Purti Tatsam presents an attractive opportunity for both end-users and investors seeking long-term value appreciation. With its spacious homes, premium amenities, and excellent connectivity, Purti Tatsam offers a compelling residential opportunity in Rajarhat, Kolkata.
