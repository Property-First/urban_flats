THE PROJECT

About Primarc Aarnya

An exclusive premium residential development by Primarc, located at Kasba Rathtala More, just off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Primarc Aarnya is a premium residential development by Primarc, thoughtfully designed for homeowners seeking spacious residences, comfort, privacy, and modern amenities. Located at Kasba Rathtala More, just beside Kasba Rathtala Mini Bus Stand, the project enjoys a strategic location with Acropolis Mall just around 8 minutes away, offering convenient access to shopping, dining, entertainment, healthcare, and everyday essentials.

Spread across 5.6 acres of freehold land, Primarc Aarnya comprises four premium towers with a G+23 elevation. The development features approximately 275 residences, offering spacious 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5 and 5 BHK simplex residences with thoughtfully planned layouts ranging from approximately 1,800 to 3,500 SBU.

Designed for an elevated lifestyle, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height, independent covered parking, and spacious configurations. Select residences also offer servant quarters, while the development incorporates a dedicated G+4 parking structure and a podium level designed to enhance the overall residential experience.

One of the distinctive highlights of Primarc Aarnya is its thoughtfully planned landscape and open spaces. The property features three ponds spread across approximately 1.5 acres, along with a podium of approximately 27,000 sq. ft.. Every flat is designed with a triple-height terrace, while the top floor incorporates lush greenery, creating a distinctive blend of architecture and nature.

The project further enhances convenience with a gas bank within the property and two dedicated entry and exit points. Towers 1, 2 and 3 feature four residences per floor, while the premium Tower 4 offers a more exclusive configuration with three residences per floor. With its spacious homes, premium planning, landscaped surroundings, and strategic location off EM Bypass, Primarc Aarnya offers an attractive residential opportunity in Kolkata.
