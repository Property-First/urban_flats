THE PROJECT

About Ambuja The Park Unizen

An ultra-luxury residential development by Ambuja, located near Bartaman Bhawan on EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Ambuja The Park Unizen is an exclusive ultra-luxury residential development by Ambuja, thoughtfully designed for discerning homeowners who seek expansive living spaces, privacy, and uncompromising luxury. Strategically located near Bartaman Bhawan on EM Bypass, the project offers seamless connectivity to Kolkata’s premier business districts, educational institutions, healthcare centres, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs.

Spread across 3.3 acres of freehold land, The Park Unizen comprises two elegant towers with a B+G+20 elevation. With only 69 exclusive residences, including spacious 4 BHK simplex apartments and grand 5 BHK duplex residences, the development offers a rare low-density living experience in one of Kolkata’s most sought-after locations.

Every residence has been crafted with exceptional attention to detail, featuring a generous 11.5-foot ceiling height, expansive layouts, premium finishes, and independent parking. The project also includes servant quarters with attached servant toilets (SQ + ST), ensuring enhanced functionality and convenience for modern luxury living.

The defining highlight of the development is its dedicated private lift and exclusive lobby for every individual residence, providing unparalleled privacy, security, and exclusivity. Thoughtfully planned amenities and premium infrastructure complement the project, creating an elevated lifestyle environment for residents.

With its prestigious EM Bypass address, limited inventory, expansive residences, and exceptional private living experience, Ambuja The Park Unizen stands as one of Kolkata’s most exclusive luxury residential opportunities for both end-users and long-term investors.
