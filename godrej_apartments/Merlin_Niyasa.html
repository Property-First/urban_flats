THE PROJECT

About Merlin Niyasa

A premium luxury residential development by Merlin Group, located behind Ruby Hospital on EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Merlin Niyasa is an exclusive luxury residential development by Merlin Group, offering a refined blend of spacious living, contemporary architecture, and premium lifestyle amenities in one of Kolkata's most sought-after residential corridors. Strategically located behind Ruby Hospital on EM Bypass, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to major business districts, reputed educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs across the city.

Spread across 3.23 acres of freehold land, Merlin Niyasa comprises 2 premium residential towers with an impressive G+28 elevation, featuring 152 exclusive luxury residences. The project offers spacious 3.5 BHK, 4 BHK, and 4.5 BHK simplex apartments, ensuring a low-density and highly private living experience for discerning homeowners.

Designed for sophisticated urban living, every residence features independent parking, premium finished interiors, 11.3-foot ceiling height, and a dedicated servant quarter with servant toilet (SQ+ST). The development also includes a landscaped podium with a clubhouse and a curated collection of lifestyle amenities for residents.

One of the project's most distinctive highlights is its 180-degree panoramic wetland view, complemented by luxurious south-facing air-conditioned residences, a unique tree house within the premises, and a Vastu-compliant North/East extended plot. These features create a serene, open, and naturally ventilated living environment rarely found in central Kolkata developments.

With an approximate fit-out timeline of December 2029 and RERA completion by June 2030, Merlin Niyasa presents a rare opportunity to own a landmark luxury residence on EM Bypass. Its prime location, limited inventory, expansive layouts, and thoughtfully curated amenities make it an exceptional choice for premium homebuyers and long-term investors.

MASTER PLAN

Thoughtfully Designed, Down to the Layout

Explore the master plan of Merlin Niyasa, EM Bypass.

Merlin Niyasa is spread across 3.23 acres of freehold land and features 2 premium residential towers with an impressive G+28 elevation. Designed for privacy, luxury, and spacious urban living, the project comprises only 152 exclusive residences, creating a low-density residential environment in one of Kolkata's most desirable EM Bypass locations.

The development includes G+3 dedicated parking levels, an elevated 4th-floor landscaped podium with clubhouse amenities, and residential floors rising up to the 28th level. Spacious 3.5 BHK, 4 BHK, and 4.5 BHK simplex residences, 11.3-foot ceiling heights, independent parking, and a dedicated servant quarter with servant toilet (SQ+ST) in every residence ensure an elevated living environment that combines exclusivity with everyday comfort.

The master plan is further enhanced by premium lifestyle amenities including a lap pool, feature cabana, tennis court, jogging and bicycle routes, and a co-working pavilion. With landscaped open spaces, a unique tree house within the premises, and 180-degree panoramic wetland views, Merlin Niyasa offers a thoughtfully planned luxury living experience rarely found in central Kolkata.
