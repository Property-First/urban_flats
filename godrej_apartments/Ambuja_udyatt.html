THE PROJECT

About Ambuja Udyatt

A boutique luxury residential development by Ambuja, located on EM Bypass before Siddha Sky, Kolkata.

Ambuja Udyatt is an exclusive luxury residential development by Ambuja, offering an exceptional blend of privacy, spacious living, and contemporary design in one of Kolkata's most promising premium corridors. Strategically located on EM Bypass before Siddha Sky, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to major business districts, reputed educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs across the city.

Spread across 2.6 acres of freehold land, Ambuja Udyatt is an iconic single-tower development with a striking G+31 elevation. The tower features 94 exclusive residences, including spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex homes, ensuring a low-density and highly private living experience for discerning homeowners.

Designed for refined urban living, the project offers a 30,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, a 4,000 sq. ft. banquet hall with terrace, and sky-level recreation floors on the 30th and 31st levels. Every residence includes a dedicated room for domestic help, independent parking, and premium finishing options, with a generous 10.5-foot floor-to-floor ceiling height that enhances space, light, and comfort.

One of the project's most distinctive features is its approximately 50 cottah lake, creating a serene environment rarely found within city developments. Combined with its boutique character, standalone presence, and significant surrounding open area, Ambuja Udyatt offers excellent long-term appreciation potential.

With an approximate fit-out timeline of mid-2029, Ambuja Udyatt presents a rare opportunity to own a landmark luxury residence on EM Bypass. Its prime location, limited inventory, and thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities make it an exceptional choice for premium homebuyers and long-term investors.

Designed for privacy, luxury, and a boutique residential experience, the project offers only 94 premium residences, creating a rare low-density living environment on EM Bypass.
