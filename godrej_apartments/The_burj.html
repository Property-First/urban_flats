THE PROJECT

About The Burj

A landmark luxury residential development by ATK Kalim Group, located at Topsia More, Kolkata.

The Burj is an exclusive luxury residential development by ATK Kalim Group, designed for homebuyers who seek privacy, spacious living, and elevated urban luxury. Strategically located at Topsia More, Kolkata, the project offers excellent connectivity to EM Bypass, Park Circus, Ballygunge, Salt Lake, and Kolkata’s major business, healthcare, educational, and lifestyle destinations.

Built on 70 Cottah of freehold land, The Burj features an exclusive single-tower development with a G+23 elevation. With only 76 premium residences, including spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex apartments, the project offers a low-density and highly private living environment.

Designed for refined contemporary living, the residences feature finished interiors, a generous 10-foot ceiling height, and thoughtfully planned layouts that maximize natural light, ventilation, and overall comfort. Residents can choose between mechanical parking and open car parking options, while servant quarters (SQ + ST) are provided for enhanced convenience.

One of the project’s standout highlights is its spectacular 15th-floor floating terrace clubhouse with a rooftop swimming pool, offering a truly elevated lifestyle experience. The development also includes comprehensive lifestyle amenities, landscaped open spaces, recreational zones, fitness facilities, and leisure areas designed for residents of all age groups.

With approximately 70% open space, a prime Topsia location, limited inventory, premium specifications, and iconic architectural appeal, The Burj presents an exceptional opportunity for discerning homebuyers and long-term investors seeking a prestigious luxury address in Kolkata.
