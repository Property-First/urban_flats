THE PROJECT

About Bhawani Paraiso

An exclusive premium residential development by Bhawani, located at Jagardanga Crossing, Rajarhat, Kolkata.

Bhawani Paraiso is a premium residential development by Bhawani, thoughtfully designed for homeowners seeking spacious residences, modern comforts, privacy, and a well-connected lifestyle. Located at Jagardanga Crossing in Rajarhat, the project enjoys a strategic position on the Rajarhat 6-lane crossing, offering convenient connectivity to key destinations across New Town and the wider Kolkata region.

Spread across 2.7 acres of freehold land, Bhawani Paraiso comprises three premium towers with a B+G+18 elevation. The development features approximately 307 residences, offering spacious 2, 3, 3.5 and 4 BHK simplex residences with thoughtfully planned layouts ranging from approximately 1,068 to 2,271 sq. ft.

Designed for comfortable modern living, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height and well-planned configurations. The project offers multiple parking options, including MLCP, dependent parking, basement parking, and ground & podium parking, depending on the residence. The development is planned to provide residents with a comfortable and convenient residential environment.

One of the distinctive highlights of Bhawani Paraiso is its extensive lifestyle amenity offering. The development features a three-level clubhouse complemented by 40+ modern amenities, including a swimming pool, home theatre, crèche, coffee house, and several recreational and lifestyle facilities designed for residents of different age groups.

With its strategic location at Jagardanga Crossing, premium residential configurations, thoughtfully planned amenities, and excellent connectivity along the Rajarhat 6-lane corridor, Bhawani Paraiso presents an attractive opportunity for homebuyers looking for a modern residential address in Rajarhat, Kolkata.
