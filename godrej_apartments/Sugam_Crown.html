THE PROJECT

About Sugam Crown at Aqua View

A premium residential development by Sugam Homes, Purti Homes, Diamond Group, and Teej Group, located in Mahishbathan off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Sugam Crown at Aqua View is a premium residential development by Sugam Homes, Purti Homes, Diamond Group, and Teej Group, thoughtfully designed for modern families seeking spacious homes, abundant open spaces, and a lifestyle enriched with luxury amenities. Strategically located in Mahishbathan, off EM Bypass, Kolkata, the project offers excellent connectivity to Sector V, New Town, Salt Lake, and other major commercial and lifestyle destinations across the city.

Spread across 4.4 acres of freehold land, Crown at Aqua View comprises five high-rise towers with G+29 and G+30 elevations, offering a total of 528 premium residences. The project features spacious 3 and 4 BHK simplex apartments with well-planned layouts, contemporary architecture, and premium specifications that cater to refined urban living.

One of the project’s most distinctive features is that approximately 3.5 acres are dedicated to landscaped open spaces, creating a low-density residential environment with abundant greenery and recreational areas. Residents will enjoy a thoughtfully designed lifestyle with 10-foot ceiling heights, premium marble flooring, and VRV air-conditioning infrastructure included as part of the development specifications.

The defining highlight of the development is its landmark skywalk connecting Towers 1–4 on the 29th floor, complemented by a 13–15 ft elevated jogging track and a range of modern lifestyle amenities. The proposed connector road linking Sector V and EM Bypass near ITC further enhances the project’s accessibility and long-term investment potential.

With its strategic EM Bypass location, expansive residences, premium infrastructure, and thoughtfully curated amenities, Sugam Crown at Aqua View stands out as one of Kolkata’s most attractive luxury residential opportunities for both discerning homebuyers and long-term investors.
