THE PROJECT

About Merlin Rise

A landmark township development by Merlin Group, located at Rajarhat Chowmatha, Kolkata.

Merlin Rise is a large-scale residential township by Merlin Group, thoughtfully designed for homebuyers seeking spacious homes, modern amenities, green open spaces, and excellent connectivity. Located at Rajarhat Chowmatha, Kolkata, the project enjoys a strategic location with seamless access to New Town, Sector V, EM Bypass, the airport, schools, hospitals, shopping destinations, and major commercial hubs.

Spread across 45 acres of freehold land, Merlin Rise comprises 8 premium residential towers with a 2B+G+27 elevation. The development features approximately 2,358 residences, offering well-planned 2 and 3 BHK finished apartments with efficient layouts ranging from approximately 632 to 1,187 sq. ft..

Designed for comfortable urban living, the residences feature a generous 10-foot ceiling height, quality finishes, and thoughtfully designed interiors. The project also offers multiple parking options, including dependent and independent covered parking, along with a well-planned residential layout that enhances everyday convenience.

One of the defining highlights of Merlin Rise is its exceptional lifestyle infrastructure. The township features over 70% open green space, landscaped podiums, expansive outdoor recreational areas, and a magnificent 78,000 sq. ft. clubhouse. Residents can enjoy premium sports facilities including a cricket ground, football field, fitness and wellness zones, indoor games, children's play areas, and community spaces.

Launched in November 2023, Merlin Rise is scheduled for RERA completion by December 2027, with an expected fit-out timeline around October 2027. With its expansive township planning, premium amenities, landscaped surroundings, and strategic Rajarhat location, Merlin Rise offers an outstanding residential opportunity for both end-users and long-term investors in Kolkata.

Thoughtfully Planned for Township Living

Explore the master plan of Merlin Rise, Rajarhat Chowmatha, Kolkata.

Merlin Rise is spread across 45 acres of freehold land and comprises 8 premium residential towers with a 2B+G+27 elevation.
