THE PROJECT

About Eden Group Vayu

A premium residential development by Eden Group, located on VIP Road in Kaikhali, near Kolkata Airport.

Eden Group Vayu is a premium residential development by Eden Group, designed for homebuyers seeking comfortable living spaces, modern amenities, and excellent connectivity. Located at Kaikhali on VIP Road, near Kolkata Airport, the project enjoys convenient access to VIP Road, Jessore Road and Metro connectivity, making it well connected to key destinations across Kolkata.

Spread across 2.46 acres of freehold land, Eden Group Vayu comprises a single premium tower with a G+12 elevation. The development offers a total of 236 residences, comprising spacious 2, 3 and 4 BHK simplex residences. The residences are planned in multiple sizes, ranging from approximately 981 to 1,916 sq. ft., catering to different family requirements.

The project offers thoughtfully planned homes with a generous 10-foot ceiling height and multiple parking options, including mechanical, open and covered parking. The development is designed to provide residents with a comfortable and convenient lifestyle while maintaining easy access to major roads and transportation networks.

Eden Group Vayu features a range of lifestyle amenities designed around the needs of modern residents. The development includes a swimming pool and multipurpose court on the podium, along with a dedicated sky lounge. A temple in the north-east zone further adds to the thoughtfully planned environment of the project.

One of the key advantages of Eden Group Vayu is its strategic location near Kolkata Airport. With access to VIP Road and Jessore Road and nearby metro connectivity, residents can enjoy convenient connectivity to major residential, commercial and transportation hubs. With its compact premium development, well-planned residences, lifestyle amenities and airport-centric location, Eden Group Vayu presents an attractive residential opportunity in Kaikhali, Kolkata.
