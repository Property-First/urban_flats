THE PROJECT

About Supreme Residency

A premium residential development by Supreme Sandy Infra Pvt. Ltd., located at Gopalpur 1, Rajarhat, after Purti Aroma.

Supreme Residency is a thoughtfully planned residential development by Supreme Sandy Infra Pvt. Ltd., designed for homebuyers seeking comfortable living, practical layouts, and essential lifestyle amenities. Located at Gopalpur 1, Rajarhat, after Purti Aroma, the project enjoys a well-connected location with convenient access to public transportation and major destinations in the Rajarhat area.

Spread across approximately 0.77 acres of freehold land, Supreme Residency comprises two residential blocks. Block A features a G+7 elevation, while Block B rises to G+5. The development offers approximately 95 residential units comprising well-planned 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK simplex residences.

The residences are designed with practical layouts and sizes ranging from approximately 866 to 1,157 sq. ft., catering to different family requirements. The homes feature a generous 9'8" ceiling height and finished interiors with vitrified tiles, creating a comfortable and contemporary living environment.

Supreme Residency also provides essential lifestyle amenities for residents, including a swimming pool, kids' play area and dedicated joggers' track. Parking provisions are available across different configurations, including covered dependent, open independent and covered independent parking, depending on the apartment type.

One of the key advantages of Supreme Residency is its strategic location, with the project positioned near the airport and on a bus route, offering convenient connectivity for daily commuting. Launched in May 2024, the project has an expected RERA completion date of October 2026. With limited residences, practical configurations, lifestyle amenities, and a freehold land parcel, Supreme Residency presents a well-rounded residential opportunity in Rajarhat, Kolkata.
