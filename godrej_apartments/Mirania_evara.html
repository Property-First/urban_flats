THE PROJECT

About Mirania Evara

An exclusive boutique luxury residential development by Mirania Group, located on EM Bypass opposite Metro Cash & Carry, Kolkata.

Mirania Evara is a premium boutique residential development by Mirania Group, crafted for homebuyers who value privacy, spacious living, and refined contemporary design. Strategically located on EM Bypass opposite Metro Cash & Carry, the project enjoys excellent connectivity to Kolkata’s major business districts, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, shopping destinations, and entertainment hubs.

Built on 52 Kattha of freehold land, Mirania Evara features an exclusive single-tower development with a B+G+18 elevation. With only 86 premium residences, including spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex apartments, the project offers a low-density and highly private living experience.

Designed for elevated urban living, the project showcases Italian marble flooring, premium VRV air-conditioning fittings, and a generous 10-foot floor-to-floor ceiling height that enhances space, natural light, and overall comfort. Residents also benefit from independent parking, while dedicated servant quarters are available with select 4 BHK residences.

The development offers thoughtfully curated lifestyle amenities including a double-height banquet hall with rooftop amenities, infinity swimming pool with submerged lounge, co-working space, yoga lawn, indoor games arena, kids’ play area, BBQ counter, and a senior citizen’s corner, creating a complete luxury living experience within a boutique community.

With its prime EM Bypass location, limited inventory, premium specifications, and thoughtfully planned amenities, Mirania Evara presents an exceptional opportunity for discerning homebuyers and long-term investors seeking a landmark luxury address in Kolkata.
