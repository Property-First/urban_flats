THE PROJECT

About Ambuja Utpalaa The Condoville

A landmark luxury township by Ambuja, located Off EM Bypass, just 5 minutes from Fortis Hospital and CIS, Kolkata.

Ambuja Utpalaa The Condoville is a premium luxury residential township by Ambuja, designed for homebuyers who seek expansive living spaces, lush green surroundings, and world-class lifestyle amenities. Strategically located Off EM Bypass, just 5 minutes from Fortis Hospital and CIS, the project enjoys seamless connectivity to Kolkata’s major business districts, educational institutions, healthcare hubs, shopping destinations, and entertainment centers.

Spread across 10.58 acres of freehold land, the development features 6 premium towers with a B+G+27 elevation, offering a carefully planned collection of 576 residences, including 68 exclusive duplex homes. The project offers spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex and duplex apartments, designed to provide privacy, abundant natural light, and generous living areas.

Crafted with premium finishes and modern architecture, the residences feature 10.5-foot ceiling heights, expansive layouts, and elegant interiors that create a sense of openness and luxury. Larger 3 BHK, 4 BHK, and duplex units are thoughtfully designed with servant quarters, while residents benefit from basement parking and superior construction quality synonymous with the Ambuja brand.

One of the project’s standout features is its extensive lifestyle infrastructure, including 5.43 acres of landscaped greens, a 3.5-acre central podium, and a 50,000 sq.ft exclusive clubhouse. Residents can enjoy a wide range of premium amenities such as a swimming pool, fitness center, indoor and outdoor sports facilities, landscaped gardens, children’s play zones, community spaces, and recreational areas that cater to every age group.

The development is also IGBC Pre-Certified Gold Rated and overlooks a natural waterbody and open green spaces, offering a rare combination of sustainability, serenity, and urban convenience. With its prime EM Bypass location, expansive township planning, premium amenities, and trusted Ambuja legacy, Ambuja Utpalaa The Condoville presents an exceptional opportunity for luxury homebuyers and long-term investors seeking a prestigious residential address in Kolkata.

MASTER PLAN

Thoughtfully Designed, Down to the Layout

Explore the master plan of Ambuja Utpalaa The Condoville, Off EM Bypass, Kolkata.

Ambuja Utpalaa The Condoville is spread across 10.58 acres of freehold land and features a thoughtfully planned luxury township comprising 6 premium towers with a B+G+27 elevation. Designed to maximize open spaces, privacy, natural light, and ventilation, the project offers 576 residences, including 68 exclusive duplex homes, creating a refined and spacious residential environment on the EM Bypass corridor.

The development includes spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK simplex and duplex residences, basement parking, and 10.5-foot ceiling heights that enhance openness and comfort across every home. Larger residences are also designed with servant quarters, while the tower placement and internal planning ensure excellent privacy, cross-ventilation, and efficient space utilization throughout the township.

The master plan is further enhanced by 5.43 acres of landscaped greens, a 3.5-acre central podium, and a 50,000 sq.ft exclusive clubhouse, along with a comprehensive range of lifestyle amenities including a swimming pool, fitness center, indoor and outdoor sports facilities, children’s play areas, wellness zones, landscaped gardens, and community spaces. The project also overlooks a natural waterbody and expansive open green spaces, offering a serene and luxurious living experience in one of Kolkata’s most connected residential destinations.
