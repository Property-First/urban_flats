Specifications

Infrastructure

Roads built with hand-crafted cobblestones, and pedestrian pathways in precast concrete flagstones. Curbs and sidewalks beside the roads in the project. Street lighting with LED lights on MS poles.

Security

24/7 security with RFID boom barriers. Boundary walls in masonry or concrete to a height of 1.8m. CCTV surveillance at all major vantage points.

Green Features

Extensive project-wide parks and landscape development with a central boulevard and over 8 acres of eucalyptus forest. Combination of trees, shrubs, and creepers based on the theme of the project.

Energy and Power

Power connection of 4 kVA for each plot of up to 2,500 sq.ft. and 5kVA for plots larger than 2,500 sq.ft. Underground power lines to distribute power through feeder pillar.

Plumbing & Water Conservation

Water supply through a combination of overhead storage with gravity distribution and a hydropneumatic pump system. Percolation pits at regular intervals within the stormwater drainage system. A tertiary sewage treatment plant with separate plumbing for the use of recycled water in landscaping.
