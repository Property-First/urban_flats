Master Plan & Floor Plans

The master plan of L&T Sarjapur Road has been meticulously designed to create a harmonious living environment that balances modern amenities with natural surroundings. The project features thoughtfully planned residential towers, extensive green spaces, and state-of-the-art amenities.

Key features of the master plan include strategically positioned residential towers to maximize views and natural light, ample green spaces and landscaped gardens throughout the property, dedicated areas for recreational activities and community gatherings, well-planned road networks and pedestrian pathways for easy navigation, central clubhouse and amenity areas conveniently located for all residents, and secure boundary walls with controlled entry-exit points for enhanced safety.

The master plan also incorporates sustainable design principles, including rainwater harvesting systems, solar energy utilization, and waste management facilities, aligning with L&T's commitment to environmental responsibility.
