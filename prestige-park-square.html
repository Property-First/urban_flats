Prestige Park Square Resale presents an exclusive collection of premium residences in the heart of Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore. This prestigious development by Prestige Group spans 8.2 acres with 586 meticulously designed apartments across 3 towers.

Experience refined urban living with spacious 2 & 3 BHK configurations ranging from 1115 sq.ft. to 1855 sq.ft., featuring luxurious finishes and smart layouts. The property boasts exceptional connectivity to NICE Road Junction while offering a serene retreat with beautifully landscaped gardens and world-class amenities.
