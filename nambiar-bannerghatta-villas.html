About Nambiar Bannerghatta Villas

Nambiar Bannerghatta Villas is the landmark new launch from Nambiar Builders on Bannerghatta Road, South Bengaluru. Spread across an expansive 70 acres, this premium gated villa community is thoughtfully designed to blend modern architecture with nature-inspired living.

The Nambiar Bannerghatta project features spacious luxury villas surrounded by lush greenery, landscaped open spaces, and excellent connectivity to key hubs of Bengaluru. Ideal for both end-users and long-term investors seeking premium villas in South Bengaluru.

With Phase 1 spanning 32 acres, this Nambiar Bannerghatta gated community offers low-density living with privacy and open spaces, creating the perfect balance of modern lifestyle and nature.
