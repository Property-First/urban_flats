About Godrej Whitefield Reimagined

Godrej Whitefield Reimagined is an upcoming high-rise residential development by Godrej Properties, coming up at Budigere Cross in Whitefield, East Bangalore. Spread across approximately 28 acres, the project brings together 7 towers offering a thoughtfully designed mix of 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments built around contemporary architecture and abundant natural light.

Set in one of Bangalore's most established IT corridors, the project is built for residents who want easy access to major tech parks and business hubs while still coming home to a calm, green, well-planned community with resort-style amenities and modern conveniences.
