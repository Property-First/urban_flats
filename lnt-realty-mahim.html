Project Overview

L&T Island Cove is the new luxury sea-facing residential apartment project launched at Mahim West, Mumbai.

Spanning approximately 2 acres of prime land, this residential enclave features the very best in L&T Realty's luxury living segment. The project offers spacious 2 & 3 BHK apartments with decks, boasting unobstructed views of the Arabian Sea and Bandra-Worli Sea Link.

As Mahim's first modern gated community, L&T Island Cove combines serene coastal living with urban convenience. Located on Mori Road near St. Michael's Church, the project offers easy access to major business districts while providing a peaceful residential environment.

L&T Realty brings its trusted legacy of quality construction and innovative design to create a landmark residential destination in central Mumbai, offering residents a perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and connectivity.
