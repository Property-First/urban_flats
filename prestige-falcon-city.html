Prestige Falcon City is a premium residential development located in the rapidly growing Kanakapura Road corridor of Bangalore. This expansive project offers meticulously designed residences with modern amenities and lush green surroundings. The development features contemporary architecture with high-quality finishes, spacious layouts, and excellent connectivity to key areas of the city.

Developed by Prestige Group, one of India's most respected real estate developers, Falcon City represents quality living with its thoughtfully planned residences, extensive amenities, and strategic location in one of Bangalore's most promising neighborhoods.
