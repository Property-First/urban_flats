Project Overview

Total Environment Over the Rainbow Villas is the new ultra-luxury residential villa project launched at the foot of Nandi Hills, North Bangalore.

The residential enclave TE Over the Rainbow is spread across 8.8 acres and features the very best in Total Environment's ultra-luxury living segment. The project offers just 33 spacious Earth-sheltered 3-bedroom villas with luxurious features.

Beautiful landscapes with expansive 2 private gardens, decks, a private pool for each villa, and plenty of open spaces all around Total Environment Over the Rainbow Villas make it more special & Elite.

The builder is known to bring a signature quality, luxurious living experience to the community of Nandi Hills near Devanahalli, North Bangalore, with each villa built on a massive 6000sft plot. The stepped arrangement of villas ensures that each villa has maximum ventilation, natural light, and uninterrupted views at TE Over the Rainbow.

Specifications

Structure

RCC frame structure with masonry partitions. High-quality exposed brick masonry using specially procured high-strength terracotta wire-cut bricks/stone masonry. Internal walls: terracotta blocks, table molded brick masonry or Aerocon blocks / Concrete block masonry.

Flooring

Beige colored imported marble laid with paper joints. Kota stone laid with paper joints and finished with 8 coats of polish. Cut-pile carpet. Imported marble on the floor and counter, Marazzi or equivalent tiles on the walls up to 7' in the shower area and 3' in other areas.

Doors & Windows

Door and window frames and shutters shall be in Engineered Solid Wood. All door shutters shall be in solid-core flush (block board or honeycomb board) shutters provided with a natural wood veneer. All bathroom windows, external utility fenestration, and the window shall have aluminum frames and shutters.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Western style, porcelain EWC of Kohler or equivalent make in all bathrooms. All faucets shall be CP, heavy body metal fittings of Grohe or equivalent make. All water supply lines & drainage lines shall be in PVC pipes. Sewage lines shall be in stoneware. Rainwater Harvesting drain pipes included.

Security

The home-automation system shall be provided with a main console/terminal with color display control with a touch screen for controlling all the automated functions. 24×7 security personnel and gated compound facility for the entire project. CCTV Cameras at all vantage points.

Electrical

All wiring shall be of Havells or equivalent make, concealed in PVC conduits. All switches shall be of Crabtree or equivalent make. Back-up power shall be provided for all common areas and provision for backup for all villas.
