Project Overview

Nambiar Ellegenza Phase 2 is the new phase of the landmark residential villa project launched on Muthanallur Road, Kaggalipura off Sarjapur Road, Bangalore. This luxurious residential enclave spans across a sprawling 17.8 acres of prime land and features the very best in Nambiar Builders' luxury segment.
