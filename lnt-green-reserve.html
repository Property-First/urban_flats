Prime Location Advantage

Connectivity & Accessibility

L&T Green Reserve is located in one of the most developing zones of Noida, near the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway. This location is well connected with other parts of Delhi NCR, particularly Noida, Greater Noida and Delhi.

As Sector 128 is located on the opposite side, and it is quite close to commercial centres, schools, colleges, hospitals, and business and entertainment areas, it provides a perfect blend of commercial and residential facilities.

  • Noida Expressway: Easy access to business areas of Noida and Greater Noida
  • Metro Access: Noida Sector 137 metro station nearby
  • Educational Institutions: Step by Step School, Genesis Global School
  • Healthcare: Jaypee Hospital, Felix Hospital in proximity
