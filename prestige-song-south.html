Project Gallery

Prestige Song of the South Resale is a brand new residential apartment project by Prestige Constructions in the rapidly expanding neighborhood of Begur, Bannerghatta Road, Bangalore. The residential enclave features the very best in Prestige Constructions luxury segment.

Beautiful landscapes surround Prestige Song of the South Resale, creating a refreshing retreat while maintaining excellent connectivity to Bangalore's conveniences. The development features brilliant architecture and quality living experience with state-of-the-art amenities.
