About Godrej Raipur Plots

A premier plotted development by India's most trusted real estate brand

Godrej Raipur is an upcoming premium residential plots project by Godrej Properties situated on Old Dhamtari Road, Raipur, Chhattisgarh. Spread across 50 acres, the development features approximately 750 freehold residential plots in a forest-themed gated community. Godrej Raipur plots allow buyers full design freedom — choose your own architect and build a fully personalized villa that reflects your lifestyle.

Located in the capital region of Chhattisgarh, Godrej Raipur sits at the junction of excellent infrastructure and natural serenity. Old Dhamtari Road is rapidly emerging as Raipur's most coveted residential corridor, offering seamless connectivity to NH-30, NH-53, the Raipur–Bilaspur Expressway, and Swami Vivekananda Airport. With Raipur's metro rail project underway and Naya Raipur's IT-SEZ maturing, the Godrej Raipur location promises strong long-term value appreciation.

Backed by 127 years of the Godrej legacy, this plotted community combines eco-friendly planning with modern infrastructure — making Godrej Raipur ideal for families seeking a green, secure, and future-ready address in Chhattisgarh.
