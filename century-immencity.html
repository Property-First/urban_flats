About Century Immencity

Century Immencity redefines grandeur in North Bangalore. Spread across a lush 50-acre parcel in the serene neighborhood of Jakkur, this upcoming luxury township features 14 elegant towers (3B+G+12F) offering expansive 3 & 4 BHK apartments ranging from 2400 to 4000 sq. ft. Designed for discerning families, the residences boast modern fluid layouts, high-end finishes, and panoramic views of verdant landscapes. With a strategic location near the international airport, tech parks, and social infrastructure, Immencity delivers a perfect balance of tranquility and urban connectivity. The project is currently in pre-launch phase with RERA approval underway, promising possession by December 2030.
