Project Overview

Welcome to L&T Thanisandra, a premium residential development by L&T Realty in the thriving locale of North Bangalore. This landmark project blends architectural excellence with nature's serenity, offering a perfect balance of comfort, luxury, and connectivity.

Designed for those who aspire to live in a modern, well-connected, and green environment, L&T Thanisandra stands as a symbol of sophistication and sustainable living.

Located in one of Bangalore's most sought-after growth corridors, Thanisandra Main Road offers seamless connectivity to major IT hubs like Manyata Tech Park, Hebbal, and Outer Ring Road. This makes it an ideal address for professionals seeking proximity to work while enjoying a peaceful, community-centric lifestyle.

With premium schools, hospitals, shopping centers, and upcoming metro connectivity nearby, this location ensures convenience at every turn.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the location advantage of L&T Thanisandra?

L&T Thanisandra is strategically located in North Bangalore with excellent connectivity to Manyata Tech Park (10 mins), Hebbal Flyover (12 mins), Kempegowda International Airport (25 mins), and upcoming metro connectivity. The location offers proximity to schools, hospitals, shopping malls, and business hubs.

What types of apartments are available at L&T Thanisandra?

The project offers premium 2 BHK (1400-1550 sq. ft.), 3 BHK (1900-2200 sq. ft.), and 4 BHK (2600-3100 sq. ft.) apartments designed with modern layouts and premium specifications.

What amenities are available at L&T Thanisandra?

The project offers world-class amenities including a clubhouse, swimming pool, fully equipped gymnasium, landscaped gardens, jogging track, sports courts, kids play area, jacuzzi, party lawn, and 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance.

Is L&T Thanisandra a sustainable project?

Yes, L&T Realty emphasizes sustainable urban design with energy-efficient systems, water conservation methods, rainwater harvesting, and eco-friendly landscaping, aligning with their vision of creating intelligent, future-ready communities.
